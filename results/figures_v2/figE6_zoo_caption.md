# Extended Data Figure 6 — caption

**The readout account across the field's model zoo.**

Forty-two frozen ImageNet networks under the rule of Fig. 6a–b: y is the input of each network's last linear layer, the 16-class shape and texture linear discriminants of y (PCA to 256 dimensions, fitted on one stratified half of the 1,200 cue-conflict images) give orthogonal shape- and texture-predictive subspaces, and logits(w) = W·(P_R y + 2w·P_S y + 2(1 − w)·P_T y) + b with the head untouched; the rate is scored on the other half of the images by the Geirhos 16-class rule (10 splits, both directions, mean). Thirty-eight are torchvision `IMAGENET1K_V1` classifiers (grey), one is the same ResNet-50 under torchvision's 2021 training recipe (orange), and three are the ResNet-50s of Geirhos et al. (2019) trained on Stylized-ImageNet (SIN), on SIN + ImageNet, and on SIN + ImageNet then fine-tuned on ImageNet (purple).

**(a)** Networks sorted by their trained rate (symbol; circles convolutional, triangles attention-based). Thick bars: the rates reachable within 5 points of the trained top-1 on a stratified 10-per-class ILSVRC-2012 validation subset (10,000 images); thin lines: the whole sweep from w = 0 to w = 1. In every ImageNet-trained network the whole sweep lies inside the gate (top-1 changes by at most 4.4 points, median 0.7), the reachable span is 0.20–0.50 (median 0.30) and the rate rises monotonically with w (ρ ≥ 0.99). Grey band, the ten human observers (0.92–0.99). The SIN-trained network has the highest trained rate of all 42 (0.81) and the smallest span (0.17; 0.70 → 0.87).

**(b)** Reachable span against trained rate over the 38 ImageNet-trained networks: the span is unrelated to the rate (Spearman ρ = +0.04) and to top-1 (−0.22), and averages 0.31 in convolutional and 0.27 in attention-based networks. Ranking by the trained rate and by the rate at the shape end of each network's gate agree at Kendall τ = 0.47; 188 of the 703 pairs (27%) reverse order. The dashed line is the pre-registered 0.20.

**(c)** The probe-based representational bias — shape minus texture accuracy of the linear probe of Methods 4.3 (PCA to 256 dimensions, multinomial logistic regression, 5-fold stratified cross-validation) on the same features — against the trained rate. Every ImageNet-trained network is texture-informative (−0.30 to −0.66) whatever its rate, and the two rankings agree only at τ = 0.44. The SIN-trained network sits apart at −0.11: its bias is in the representation, which is why the readout moves it least (a), a distinction the rate alone cannot make. Error consistency with the observers (Extended Data Table 16, not drawn) is 2–23% of the human–human ceiling at the trained readouts, rises with the rate across networks (ρ = 0.80) and within every gate (38 of 38), and reaches at most 37% at any gated readout of an ImageNet-trained network; the SIN network reaches 40% trained and 53% at its shape end.
