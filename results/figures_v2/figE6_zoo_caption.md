# Extended Data Figure 6 — caption

**The readout account across the field's model zoo, on exemplars the subspaces were not fitted on.**

Forty-two frozen ImageNet networks under the rule of Fig. 6a–b: y is the input of each network's last linear layer, the 16-class shape and texture linear discriminants of y (PCA to 256 dimensions) give orthogonal shape- and texture-predictive subspaces, and logits(w) = W·(P_R y + 2w·P_S y + 2(1 − w)·P_T y) + b with the head untouched; the rate is scored by the Geirhos 16-class rule. Because the 1,200 cue-conflict images reuse 160 object and 48 texture exemplars, the drawn estimator splits both into three folds, fits the subspaces on the images whose object and texture exemplars both lie outside one fold and scores the rate on the images whose exemplars both lie inside it (ten repetitions, mean curve; Methods 4.8). The pale bars are the pre-registered estimator of `docs/PLAN_ZOO_2026-09-15.md`, fitted on one stratified half of the images and scored on the other, which shares 98% of its object exemplars and all of its texture exemplars with the fitting half. Thirty-eight networks are torchvision `IMAGENET1K_V1` classifiers (grey), one is the same ResNet-50 under torchvision's 2021 training recipe (orange), and three are the ResNet-50s of Geirhos et al. (2019) trained on Stylized-ImageNet (SIN), on SIN + ImageNet, and on SIN + ImageNet then fine-tuned on ImageNet (purple).

**(a)** Networks sorted by their trained rate (symbol; circles convolutional, triangles attention-based), which is scored on all 1,200 images; the held-out curves are scored on the images of the held-out folds only and pass within 0.02 of it at w = 0.5. Bars: the rates reachable within 5 points of the trained top-1 on a stratified 10-per-class ILSVRC-2012 validation subset (10,000 images), with exemplars held out (dark) and shared (pale). In every network the whole sweep lies inside the gate (top-1 changes by at most 4.4 points, median 0.7, measured on the subspaces of the exemplar-shared fit, whose w-range the held-out fits inherit). With exemplars held out the reachable span of the 38 ImageNet-trained networks is 0.08–0.29 (median 0.19; 11 of the 38 reach the pre-registered 0.20), against 0.20–0.50 (median 0.30) with exemplars shared, and the held-out mean curve rises monotonically with w in all 42 networks (ρ ≥ 0.99). Grey band, the ten human observers (0.92–0.99). The SIN-trained network has the highest trained rate of all 42 (0.81) and a held-out span of 0.12 (0.73 → 0.85), the 3rd smallest of 39; with exemplars shared its span is 0.17 (0.70 → 0.87), and on the forced two-way estimator 0.32, the 16th smallest (Extended Data Tables 16 and 18).

**(b)** Held-out gated span against trained rate over the 38 ImageNet-trained networks: the span is unrelated to the rate (Spearman ρ = +0.10; +0.04 with exemplars shared) and to top-1 (−0.27), and averages 0.19 in convolutional and 0.16 in attention-based networks. Ranking by the trained rate and by the rate at the shape end of each network's gate agree at Kendall τ = 0.63; 131 of the 703 pairs (19%) reverse order, against 8.9% [6.0, 12.2] for the trained ranking re-estimated on two independent resamples of the images (τ = 0.47 and 27% with exemplars shared). The dashed line is the pre-registered 0.20.

**(c)** The exemplar-balanced probe bias — shape minus texture accuracy of a linear probe on the same features with three exemplars per class for both labels, one exemplar left out per fold and PCA and scaling fitted inside each fold; mean of 20 draws, bars the 5–95% range over draws — against the trained rate. Thirty-seven of the 38 ImageNet-trained networks are texture-leaning (−0.52 to +0.01, the exception ViT-B/32 at +0.01 [−0.09, +0.09]), and the two rankings agree only at τ = 0.43. The SIN network is the only shape-leaning network of the 42 (+0.23 [+0.13, +0.34]): its bias is in the representation, a distinction the rate alone cannot make, and the gain moves it mainly by adding shape evidence where it moves 37 of the 38 ImageNet-trained networks mainly by removing texture evidence (Extended Data Table 18). The paper's original stratified-fold probe, whose test images share exemplars with its training folds, gives −0.30 to −0.66 for every ImageNet-trained network, −0.11 for SIN and τ = 0.44 (Extended Data Table 16). Error consistency with the observers (Extended Data Table 16, not drawn) is 2–23% of the human–human ceiling at the trained readouts, rises with the rate across networks (ρ = 0.80) and within every gate (38 of 38), and reaches at most 37% at any gated readout of an ImageNet-trained network; the SIN network reaches 40% trained and 53% at its shape end.
