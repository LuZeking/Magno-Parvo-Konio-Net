# Extended Data Figure 2 — caption

**Effective dimensionality: the diet fixes which side of parity training ends on, not a level.**

Ratio of the participation ratio of the magno stream's features to the parvo stream's, computed on 1,000 NSD images per subject (n = 8; Methods). Left: each arm's front end before any training, marker at the subject mean with the subject range as a whisker. Right: every training seed at the final checkpoint, each the mean over 8 subjects, fanned from the front end it started at. The four front ends hand the network very different asymmetries before learning (0.59 to 1.91, a 3.3-fold range), including one that is inverted: in `parvo_highsf` the parvo stream is the more dimensional stream in 8/8 subjects. After training every diet-trained seed ends magno-dominant (24/24 subject-seeds above parity in each of the three diet arms) while the no-diet arm collapses to parity (20/24 at or below). No level is labelled: the within-arm seed range (up to 2.39 at the final checkpoint, 0.79 at the best saved one) exceeds the gaps between arm means, so any statement of the form “training converges to X” is unsupported at three seeds. The `parvo_highsf` case also separates dimensionality from shape bias: its second stream is the more dimensional one while remaining texture-biased.
