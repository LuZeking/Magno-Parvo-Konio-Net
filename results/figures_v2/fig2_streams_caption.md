# Figure 2 — caption

**The two streams end training on opposite sides of the shape–texture boundary, and they get there by different routes.**

**(a)** Linear-probe shape−texture bias of each stream's features (shape-label accuracy minus texture-label accuracy on the 1,200 Geirhos cue-conflict images; zero is the sign flip), before and after training, with the diet (solid) and without it (dashed). The untrained references are single models with no training seed; trained points are the mean of three seeds with the seed range as a bar. Under the diet the magno stream is already shape-biased before any weight is updated (+0.80) and training adds +0.02; the parvo stream starts near neutral and acquires its texture bias over training (−0.43). Without the diet, training drives the luminance stream from +0.42 across the sign flip to −0.23: ordinary training destroys the shape bias the input supplies. The parvo stream ends texture-biased whether or not the other stream is dieted.

**(b, c)** The carrier × transform 2×2 on the probe (b) and on cue-conflict shape bias (c). Each line is one carrier (chromatic L−M or achromatic luminance); the x-axis is whether the developmental transform is applied to it. Points are training seeds (three, or two for the achromatic/no-transform cell); the thick line is their mean. Neither factor alone crosses the boundary; only the conjunction does, on both instruments. Cells are read out on stream 2 in three cases and on the diet's magno stream in the fourth (Methods).

**(d)** How each readout decides the 1,200 cue-conflict images at the best saved checkpoint (diet) and the final checkpoint (no diet): images decided as the shape category, as the texture category, or as neither of the two. Shape bias is shape/(shape + texture); the number of decided images varies about four-fold across readouts, so rates near parity are read as “no strong preference”.

**(e, f)** Accuracy-matched control. The diet-trained models are less accurate, so the conjunction effect could in principle be an accuracy effect. A no-diet network retrained with per-epoch checkpointing and stopped at the epoch nearest the diet's accuracy (epoch 10, 0.548 against 0.556) closes 7.6% of the probe gap (e) and 7.0% of the behavioural gap (f), and its magno stream stays on the texture side. This control rests on one matched run (n = 1); the diet and no-diet arms show three seeds each.
