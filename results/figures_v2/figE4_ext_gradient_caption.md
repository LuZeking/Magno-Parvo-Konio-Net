# Extended Data Figure 4 — caption

**Beyond hV4 the gradient saturates in every direction: the texture-biased stream carries extrastriate cortex whole, ventral, lateral and dorsal alike.**

**(a)** Stream-preference index, the median over reliable voxels of (Um − Up)/(Um + Up) (noise ceiling > 0.1, Um + Up > 10⁻³, ≥ 20 voxels per ROI per subject), on the 22 ROIs of the Wang et al. (2015) atlas that pass the voxel rule in NSD's nsdgeneral mask, grouped as early (V1–hV4), ventral (VO1/2, PHC1/2), lateral (LO1/2, TO1/2) and dorsal (V3A/B, IPS0–2). Thick lines are the mean over the eight subjects, thin lines individual subjects; the trained arms are the mean over three training seeds per subject, the untrained network is one seed on the diet front end. Under the diet the index falls from −0.05 in V1v to −0.81 in hV4 and then sits between −0.80 and −0.94 in every extrastriate ROI. Without the diet the index is +0.80 to +1.00 everywhere. The same saturation holds on NSD's stream atlas and on the face-, place-, body- and word-selective fLoc ROIs (Extended Data Table 11).

**(b)** The two components of the index, both normalised by the noise ceiling: unique variance of the parvo band (Up, circles, solid) and of the magno band (Um, squares, dashed), median over the same voxels. Under the diet Up rises monotonically from 0.10 (V1v) to 0.26 (PHC2) while Um collapses from 0.09 to about 0.01 beyond V3; without the diet the luminance stream shows the same rise (0.18 to 0.27) and the L−M stream's unique variance stays near 0.01 throughout. The rise toward higher areas therefore belongs to whichever stream carries the full-resolution image; the diet moves it from the luminance stream to the chromatic one.

**(c)** Regional contrasts per subject (points) with the mean as a bar. Left of the divider: each region's mean index minus the V1 mean, which is large and negative under the diet in all three directions (ventral −0.76, lateral −0.75, dorsal −0.75; 8/8 subjects, exact Wilcoxon p = .0078) and small and positive without it. Right of the divider: region-to-region contrasts (dorsal − ventral, lateral − ventral, LO − hV4), none of which departs from zero under either arm (all |Δ| < 0.06, p > .1). The model reproduces no magnocellular preference of dorsal cortex and no shape-selective reversal in LO.
