# Figure 4 — caption

**Behaviour reads out from both streams, and the shape-bias rate is a property of that readout.**

**(a)** Cue-conflict shape bias as a function of w, the weight on the magno stream in the readout full = classifier(w·y₁ + (1−w)·y₂), at 21 values of w, for three diet-trained seeds under both checkpoint policies. Thin lines are seeds, thick lines the seed mean. Nothing is retrained, refitted or reselected along the x-axis: y₁ and y₂ do not depend on w, so the sweep moves the readout while both representations stay bit-identical. Shape bias runs from 0.40 to 0.90 at the best saved checkpoints; w = 0.5 is the trained model and reproduces its published per-seed values exactly. The ten human observers who viewed the identical images (mean 0.958, range 0.919–0.987; grey band) and the three CNNs of the Geirhos et al. (2018a) release are drawn on the same axis. No value of w reaches the lowest individual observer.

**(b)** The span is not a property of the affine head. Span of shape bias over w (max − min) per seed, best saved checkpoints, for the frozen affine head of (a), a linear head refitted on the frozen features at each w, and a two-layer nonlinear head refitted likewise (identical budget, same initialisation seed at every w). Median spans 0.508, 0.461 and 0.466; the refitted-linear and nonlinear heads agree to within 1%. Dashed bars: the span restricted to readouts within 5 points of peak validation accuracy (0.283 for the nonlinear head), the range over which the rate can be moved without buying it with accuracy.

**(c)** Where every model sits: cue-conflict shape bias against validation accuracy at the scored checkpoint, one marker per training seed. The diet-trained network's two streams read out alone bracket its integrated readout (0.903, 0.400 and 0.655); the two single-stream controls trained with the identical diet (one ResNet-18 trunk, 11.2M parameters; one ResNet-34 trunk, 21.3M, parameter-matched to the 22.4M two-stream model) are more accurate than the two-stream model and lie at 0.567 and 0.626. The three no-diet conditions cluster near parity at 0.70 accuracy. The published CNNs were trained on ImageNet and their accuracy is not on this axis; they are drawn at the left margin for their shape bias only.
