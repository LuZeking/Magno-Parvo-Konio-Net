# Figure 1 — caption

**A two-stream network fed magnocellular- and parvocellular-like inputs, with a developmental diet on the luminance channel that matures over training.**

**(a)** The model. Each RGB image is converted to the DKL cone-opponent space and split once, before any convolution: stream 1 (“magno”) receives the luminance channel, stream 2 (“parvo”) the L−M chromatic channel. During training the luminance channel passes through the acuity blur and the contrast-sensitivity cut of the developmental visual diet of Lu et al. (2026), both evaluated at one age that rises linearly with the training step from birth to 300 months (Methods 4.1); every evaluation uses the 300-month endpoint. The two ResNet-18 trunks are initialised bit-identically, and a linear classifier reads out w·y₁ + (1−w)·y₂ of the two streams' penultimate features, with w = 0.5 during training, under one cross-entropy and no loss on either stream. The table lists the networks compared: the model, its control (the 300-month diet from the first step: the same architecture, recipe, seeds and test input, without the trajectory), and the originating study's BA network (a fixed σ = 4 px blur and a 48-month contrast cut, thesis recipe) with its no-diet arm.

**(b)** What each stream sees, for one NSD image (id 15504, the most chromatic of a fixed candidate grid), rendered with the training front end. Top: the RGB image and its luminance and L−M channels (L−M contrast-stretched for display only). Bottom: stream 1's input at three ages of the trajectory, 30, 150 and 300 months (the ends of epochs 5, 25 and 50); the contrast cut leaves almost no fine detail until the second half of training, and the 300-month endpoint is nearly clean luminance.

**(c)** Validation top-1 accuracy (MiniEcoset, 112 classes) over training. Left, the model's recipe (50 epochs): the model reaches 0.683 ± 0.002 at its final checkpoint, within 0.9 points of its best epoch in every seed, against 0.764 ± 0.003 for the control; the BA diet under the same recipe reaches 0.665 ± 0.003, and a trajectory stopping at 48 months (one seed) 0.664. The top axis gives the model's diet age. Right, the BA network under the thesis's recipe (150 epochs): 0.556 at its best saved checkpoint, after which it degrades (final 0.445), against 0.70 without the diet. Thin lines: seeds; thick: seed mean; vertical rules: saved checkpoints.
