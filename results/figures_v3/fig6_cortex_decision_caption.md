Registered design: `docs/PLAN_CORTEX_DECISION_2026-10-26.md`; reading of record `CORTEX_NOT_TEXTURE_BIASED_ON_THESE_IMAGES` (P-C1, P-C2, P-C4 not met; P-C3 met; the same in the three registered sensitivity analyses).
