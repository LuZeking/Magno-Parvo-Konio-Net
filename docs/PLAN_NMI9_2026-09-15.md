## 1. Principles

1. **Pre-specify, then run.** Each workstream below that produces a number gets a
   `docs/PLAN_<name>_<date>.md` with directional predictions and thresholds, committed
   before the job is submitted, and an Outcomes section appended afterwards. The
   referees scored this behaviour as a strength three times; it is also what lets a
   failed prediction be reported without damage.
2. **No n = 1 survives** where a seed can be run. The only permitted single-network
   cells are the thesis's own checkpoint (an independent-codebase replication, by nature
   one network) and untrained anchors that replicate across seeds trivially.
3. **Magnitude on the field's own models is the prize.** The one result that would lift
   Advance, Relevance and R2's recommendation together is a readout manipulation that
   moves the cue-conflict rate of standard ImageNet networks by ≥ 0.20 within a
   five-point accuracy budget. Everything else is packaging.
4. **Never overclaim.** If W1 fails its thresholds, the abstract keeps the "in kind"
   scoping it has now; the plan still reaches ≈ 8.8–9.0 through the other axes.
5. **One story, fewer words.** Cuts come from pointers to Extended Data, not from
   removing controls.

## 2. Workstreams

### W1 — Subspace readout re-weighting (Advance, Relevance, Evidence) — Tier 1, critical path

**Why.** The generality test factorised units by a median split of one score, which
throws away shape information that is distributed across units: 0–2% of ImageNet
units are shape-leaning in absolute terms, so the sweep had almost nothing to amplify
(ED Table 13). The v2b referee's actual question was "re-weighting of shape- and
texture-*predictive subspaces*". A subspace version is the natural strengthening, needs
no retraining, runs on stored features, and directly answers "in kind only".

**Design (to be pre-specified in `docs/PLAN_SUBSPACE_<date>.md`).**
- Features y (penultimate, D-dim) of every network already extracted
  (`/scratch/byrong/mpk/generality/features/2026-09-14_gen/`).
- On the fitting half of the cue-conflict images (stratified by shape × texture pair):
  a 16-class shape LDA and a 16-class texture LDA on y (PCA-whitened to 256 dims first
  for D > 512); take the 15 discriminant directions of each; orthogonalise the texture
  basis against the shape basis (and the reverse order as a sensitivity), giving
  projectors P_S, P_T (rank 15 each) and the residual P_R = I − P_S − P_T.
- Fixed-head gain: y(w) = P_R y + 2w·P_S y + 2(1 − w)·P_T y, through the network's own
  head; w = 0.5 is the trained network exactly. Scored on the held-out half, 10 splits,
  both directions; primary and forced estimators; accuracy gate on the same held-out
  natural images as ED Table 13 (MiniEcoset validation / ILSVRC-2012 subset).
- Lossy-sum refit analogue (our networks): z(w) = [w·s + (1 − w)·t, r] with s, t the
  15-d projections paired by discriminant rank and r the residual coordinates; linear
  and two-layer heads refitted at each w with the Methods 4.3 budget.
- Cortex: three bands S, T, R in the banded ridge model of Methods 4.5 for the seven
  networks with NSD features (rgb_tsn ×3, single34 ×3, ResNet-50), plus ViT-B/16 and
  ConvNeXt-B after NSD feature extraction (≈ 6 h CPU each); index (U_S − U_T)/(U_S + U_T);
  the residual band absorbs the shared variance that made the unit-split unique
  variances tiny. Cortex-optimal w of the lossy sum on the 11-point grid.
- Validation on the two-stream concatenation: energy of the shape basis on stream-1
  units.

**Pre-registered predictions (thresholds fixed before running).**
- P-A: gated span (within 5 points of trained top-1) ≥ 0.20 in ≥ 4 of 6 ImageNet
  networks and in all three of our single-trunk families; seed-mean ρ(w, rate) ≥ 0.90.
- P-B: ≥ 90% of the shape basis's energy lies on stream-1 units in the concatenation.
- P-C: extrastriate − V1 index negative in ≥ 7/8 subjects for rgb_tsn, single34 **and**
  ResNet-50 (the cell that failed under the unit split); cortex-optimal w lower in
  extrastriate cortex than in V1 in ≥ 7/8 for at least two of the three.
- Failure statements: if P-A fails, the abstract keeps "in kind, at a third to half of
  the two-stream span" and the title is re-examined (see §4); if P-C fails for ResNet-50,
  the cortical generality claim stays scoped to networks trained on our images.

**Compute.** Behaviour: minutes per network on CPU (`slurm/generality_cc.sh` pattern,
16G). Cortex: 3-band fits ≈ 1.5–3 h per element at ≤ 48G, 9 networks × 8 subjects = 72
elements plus 72 w-sweeps (`generality_cortex.sh` pattern) ≈ 1,500 allocated core-hours.
Wall-clock ≈ 4 days with a %14 throttle. Coding ≈ 2 days (extend `unit_factor.py`
with a `--factor lda` mode writing `B_S`, `B_T`; `cortex_bands.py` to accept three bands;
`refit_sum.py` to take the block form).

**Deliverable.** Fig. 6 redrawn around the subspace sweep (a: gated spans per network,
median split shown as the weaker rule; b: model-zoo ranking, W3; c–d: cortex). ED
Table 13/14 extended by the subspace rows. Methods 4.8 rewritten around subspaces with
the unit split as the special case.

### W3 — The field's model zoo and a measurement tool (Relevance, Advance) — Tier 1

**Why.** Relevance moved 2.3 → 3.0 on six networks in Fig. 6b. To reach 4 the ML
reader must see the consequence on the models they actually rank, and be handed the
remedy. This is cheap: feature extraction on 1,200 cue-conflict images and a 10k
ILSVRC-2012 subset per model, CPU.

**Design.**
- Models: every torchvision ImageNet classifier family (ResNet-18/34/50/101/152,
  ResNeXt-50, Wide-ResNet-50, DenseNet-121/201, VGG-11/16/19-BN, EfficientNet-B0/B3/V2-S,
  RegNet-Y-8GF, MobileNet-V3-L, ViT-B/16, ViT-B/32, ViT-L/16, Swin-T/S/B, ConvNeXt-T/S/B/L,
  MaxViT-T, Inception-v3, GoogLeNet, AlexNet; ≈ 30), plus Geirhos's Stylized-ImageNet
  ResNet-50 (SIN and SIN+IN; a *shape-biased* ImageNet network is the informative
  control: does the same gain move it *down*?), and an adversarially-trained ResNet-50
  if its weights load without new dependencies. `models.py` already loads `tv:` specs;
  add a `hub:` loader for the two Geirhos checkpoints.
- Per model: trained rate (Geirhos 16-class rule), gated span under the median split
  and under the W1 subspace rule, rank stability of the field's ordering (Kendall τ
  between trained ranking and the ranking at the largest gated w, plus the number of
  pairwise reversals), and a probe-based *representation* measure (shape − texture
  accuracy of a linear probe, our Fig. 2a instrument) so the reader sees which of the
  three quantities a "shape bias" number reports.
- Tool: `python -m src.analysis.generality.report --model tv:resnet50` prints the
  three numbers and the gated span; documented in the README; a Discussion box "How to
  report shape bias" (rate at the trained readout · reachable span within an accuracy
  budget · probe-based representational bias · error consistency where trial data
  exist).

**Prediction (pre-registered 2026-09-15 in `docs/PLAN_ZOO_2026-09-15.md` as P-Z1–P-Z4, which supersede this sketch; 42 networks incl. the three Geirhos SIN ResNet-50s and a ResNet-50 recipe variant).** Kendall τ between the trained
and readout-shifted rankings < 0.7 across the zoo; the SIN network's rate falls under
the same gain that raises the others, with its trained rate remaining the maximum.

**Compute.** ≈ 30 models × (1,200 + 10,000 images) on CPU ≈ 50 core-hours;
`slurm/generality_cc.sh` and `generality_inval.sh` patterns, 16–24G.

**Deliverable.** Fig. 6b as a zoo panel; ED Table 15 (per-model rows); README section;
one Discussion paragraph replacing the current single sentence on reporting.

### W7 — Developmental dose–response (Advance, Evidence on the neuroscience side) — Tier 2

**Why.** Limitations concede "a fixed 48-month front end rather than a trajectory".
A graded manipulation turns the diet from a transform into a developmental variable and
supplies a monotone prediction the account must survive. It is the one addition that
would let a neuroscience referee (R2) move past "existence proof".

**Design (pre-specified).** Ages {6, 12, 24, 96} months × 3 seeds (48 months and the
no-diet arm exist), same recipe, 150 epochs. Predictions: the magno stream's probe bias
and the cortical gradient hV4 − V1 decrease monotonically with age (Spearman over the
six levels < 0 in every seed), validation accuracy increases monotonically, and the
readout span of Fig. 4a is preserved at every age.

**Compute.** 12 runs × ≈ 30 GPU-hours ≈ 360 GPU-hours on A5000s; two waves of ≤ 8
concurrent runs ≈ 4–6 days wall-clock. Encoding for two intermediate ages: 48 elements
on the W2 alpha-batched path ≈ 1,150 core-hours. Total ≈ 2 weeks including analysis.

**Decision rule.** Run if the Tier-1 re-review lands below 8.8, or if GPU capacity is
idle during Tier 1 (submit the training array in week 1 so the runs finish while W1–W6
proceed; the decision to *analyse and include* is made after the re-review).

## 5. Re-review protocol

After Tier 1, re-run the `nmi-reviewer` skill on the rebuilt PDF and read the per-axis
table against §0. Targets: Advance 4 (requires P-A), Evidence 4 (requires W2a–c
complete and no "n = 1" in any weakness), Relevance 4 (W3 in a main figure), Reproducibility
4 (DOI in the PDF, notebook path named), RAI 4 (W6), Clarity 4 (≤ 5,000 words, six
items). Any axis still at 3 gets one targeted round; a second failure on the same axis
means the plan's assumption was wrong and the story is re-examined rather than padded.

## 6. What we will not do

- Rerun the pre-specified unit-split analysis until it passes; its outcome is reported
  as it fell and the subspace analysis is a new, separately pre-specified test.
- Add seeds where they cannot change a conclusion (the thesis checkpoint).
- Fit w to human observers, or claim a human operating point.
- Cut controls to reach the word limit; only prose moves to Extended Data.
- Claim robustness, human-likeness or a training recommendation for the diet (Results 2.5
  rules all three out).
