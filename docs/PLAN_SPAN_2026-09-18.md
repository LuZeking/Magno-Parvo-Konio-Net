# Pre-registration: what sets the span — rate and span as two parameters of one evidence distribution

Written 2026-09-18, 16:00, before `src/analysis/generality/span_model.py` existed or was run on
any network. Committed before the analysis. Extends `docs/PLAN_ZOO_2026-09-15.md` (W3) and
`docs/PLAN_KAPPA_2026-09-17.md`, whose networks, subspace rule, estimators and gate are used
unchanged; no network is retrained and no feature is re-extracted. This analysis is **post hoc
with respect to the manuscript's pre-registered plans** — it was prompted by the fifth referee
simulation (`docs/NMI_REVIEW_v5_2026-09-17.md`: "what sets the span", and the open requests m5
and m9) — and will be labelled post hoc wherever it is reported. What is pre-registered here is
the prediction set, written before the numbers existed.

## Question

Across the 38 ImageNet-trained networks of Extended Data Table 16 the gated span is 0.20–0.50,
and it is unrelated to the trained rate (Spearman ρ = +0.04). The manuscript reports this and
does not explain it. Two questions follow, and the stored data can answer both:

1. **What sets how far the readout can move a network's rate?** If the span is a property of the
   representation (how much of the evidence that decides a cue-conflict image runs through the
   two subspaces the gain acts on), it should be predictable from quantities measured at the
   trained readout, before any gain is applied.
2. **Why is it unrelated to where the network sits?** A single decision model can say: the rate
   is the *location* of the per-image shape-minus-texture evidence relative to its width; the
   span is the gain's *leverage* over that evidence relative to the same width. If location and
   leverage are separate properties of a network, rate and span need not covary.

## Model

For cue-conflict image i with shape category s and texture category t, the Geirhos rule scores
category c by p_c(ℓ) = mean over the ImageNet classes k ∈ c of softmax(ℓ)_k (Methods 4.8), and
the forced two-way estimator calls the image "shape" iff the **margin**
Δ_i = log p_s − log p_t is positive. Under the gain of Methods 4.8,
ℓ_i(w) = ℓ_i(½) + (2w − 1)·W(P_S − P_T)y_i exactly. The derivative of Δ_i with respect to the
logits is u_i = q_s − q_t, where q_c is the softmax restricted to the classes of c (the global
normaliser cancels in the difference). Define, at the trained readout (w = ½):

- **evidence through each subspace:** e_S,i = u_i·W P_S y_i and e_T,i = u_i·W P_T y_i;
- **leverage:** g_i = e_S,i − e_T,i, the first-order change of the margin per unit of s = 2w − 1;
- **first-order model M0:** Δ_i(w) ≈ Δ_i + (2w − 1)·g_i; predicted forced rate
  F̂(w) = mean_i 1{Δ_i + (2w − 1) g_i > 0};
- **two-moment model M1:** location **z = mean(Δ)/sd(Δ)** and leverage **λ = mean(g)/sd(Δ)**
  over the 1,200 images; predicted forced rate F̂₁(w) = Φ(z + (2w − 1)λ), trained rate Φ(z),
  and span over the gate Φ(z + s_hi λ) − Φ(z + s_lo λ), with s = 2w − 1 at the gate's ends.

M0 is a first-order rewriting of the computation that produces the rate, so its agreement with
the measured curve (S-1) is a check of the linearisation, not independent evidence. M1 is a
genuine reduction — two numbers per network in place of 1,200 margins — and the independent
content of this plan is in S-3 to S-7: which of the two numbers carries the span, whether they
are independent across networks, what they say about the Stylized-ImageNet network, whether the
leverage has a decision-free structural origin, and what a gain on directions carrying no label
information does.

**Structural predictor (decision-free).** With A the 16 × D category-contrast readout
(A = (I − 11ᵀ/16)·C·W, C averaging the head's rows over each category's ImageNet classes) and
Σ the covariance of the network's features over the 1,200 cue-conflict images (no decision, no
margin, no rate), the **head-weighted subspace share** is
H = [tr(A P_S Σ P_S Aᵀ) + tr(A P_T Σ P_T Aᵀ)] / tr(A Σ Aᵀ): the fraction of the head's
category-contrast variance carried by the two 15-dimensional subspaces. The feature-variance
share already reported (var_frac_S + var_frac_T, Extended Data Table 13) is its unweighted
analogue. The same H on the 10,000 ImageNet validation images is reported as a sensitivity, not
predicted.

## Data and targets

**Networks.** The 42 of W3 as stored in `/scratch/byrong/mpk/generality/{features,behaviour}/
2026-09-15_zoo`. Across-network statistics use the **38 ImageNet-trained** networks; the
`IMAGENET1K_V2` ResNet-50 and the three Stylized-ImageNet ResNet-50s are reported separately.

**Inputs.** Stored cue-conflict features y, head (W, b), all-image bases B_S, B_T (the bases of
the κ sweep), gate (`<tag>_gain_accuracy_subspace.json`), the in-sample rate and forced rate at
each w (`<tag>_kappa_sweep.json`), and the held-out gated span (`zoo_behaviour.json`).

**Targets.** T_f: the **forced two-way gated span**, in sample under the all-image bases — the
quantity M0 and M1 model directly. T_p: the **reported gated span** of Extended Data Table 16
(primary estimator, held out, 10 splits both directions) — the quantity the paper reports.
All 38 ImageNet networks have the whole sweep inside the gate, so for them both are the w = 1
minus w = 0 difference.

**Statistics.** The zoo is a fixed set, so across-network statistics are descriptive: Spearman ρ
with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval over networks (10,000 resamples, seed 0). Thresholds
apply to point estimates. No p-values.

## Predictions

- **S-1 (the first-order model is adequate).** Over the 38, the median of max_w |F̂(w) − F(w)|
  is ≤ 0.05, and ρ(M0 span, T_f) ≥ 0.9.
- **S-2 (two numbers suffice).** The M1 span ranks the networks' spans at ρ ≥ 0.8 against T_f
  and ρ ≥ 0.7 against T_p; and Φ(z) ranks the trained forced rates at ρ ≥ 0.9.
- **S-3 (leverage, not location, sets the span).** ρ(λ, T_p) ≥ 0.7 and |ρ(z, T_p)| ≤ 0.3.
- **S-4 (location and leverage are independent — why the span is unrelated to the rate).**
  |ρ(λ, z)| ≤ 0.3 over the 38.
- **S-5 (the Stylized-ImageNet network).** (a) Its λ is below that of every one of the 38
  ImageNet networks. (b) Of its two leverage terms, the texture term −mean(e_T) is below that of
  every ImageNet network: its span is small because its texture subspace carries little
  texture evidence to take away.
- **S-6 (a decision-free origin).** ρ(H, λ) ≥ 0.5 and ρ(H, T_p) ≥ 0.5, and H ranks T_p better
  than the unweighted share var_frac_S + var_frac_T does (by ≥ 0.10 in ρ).
- **S-7 (null for the span; review m9).** For each of the 42 networks, ten draws (seeds 0–9) of
  a pair of mutually orthogonal random 15-dimensional subspaces inside the network's own
  256-dimensional PCA space of the cue-conflict features (the space the discriminant directions
  are fitted in), orthonormalised by QR, under exactly the gain, estimator and 5-point top-1 gate
  of Methods 4.8 (in-sample rate on all 1,200 images; top-1 on the stored ImageNet validation
  subset). Predictions: the median over the 38 of |mean signed random span| ≤ 0.03, and the
  subspace-rule span exceeds the largest |random span| of the ten draws in ≥ 36 of the 38.

## Reported regardless of outcome

- Per network: z, λ, Φ(z), mean e_S, mean e_T, H (cue-conflict and ImageNet-validation Σ),

- **Zoo on the forced estimator (review m5).** The 38 networks' trained forced rates (stored

  forced rate at the shape end of the gate, and the number of reversing pairs, beside the
  primary-estimator values (τ = 0.47, 27%). Expected within 0.1 of τ = 0.47; not a prediction
  the paper depends on. Also on the in-sample primary estimator, to separate the estimator from
  the held-out split.
- ρ of λ with the probe's shape accuracy, texture accuracy and bias (Extended Data Table 16),
  with trained top-1 and with D — reported, not predicted.

## Decision rules fixed in advance

- The model is first order at w = ½ and M1 uses exactly two moments. If S-1 fails, the failure
  is reported and M1 is still evaluated as specified; a model using the exact end margins, or
  more moments, may be shown only as post hoc.
- No other target, predictor, network subset, estimator or null construction will be examined
  for the predictions. Anything else is labelled post hoc.
- **Consequences for the manuscript.** If S-3 and S-4 hold, Results 2.4 states that the rate is
  the location and the span the leverage of one evidence distribution, and that their
  independence is why the span is unrelated to the rate; Methods gains the model; the
  Discussion's reporting standard names the span as an estimate of λ. If S-3 or S-4 fails, the
  failure is reported and no such claim is made. S-5 decides whether the Stylized-ImageNet
  sentence "which is why the readout moves it least" stays as written, is sharpened to the
  texture term, or is weakened. S-7 goes into the Extended Data whatever its outcome.
