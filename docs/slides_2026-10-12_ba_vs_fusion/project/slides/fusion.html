How our fusion differs from the BA's TSN

Unchanged: two ResNet-18 trunks · same diets (blurred luminance / L−M) · streams meet only after layer 4

BA: average, then one head

z = W · (h_magno + h_parvo) / 2

  • One shared head reads the mixed features
  • One loss, so neither stream learns to classify alone
  • Adam 1e-4 constant, 150 epochs, last epoch

Ours: a head per stream, then mix

z = w · z_magno + (1 − w) · z_parvo

  • z_magno = W_m h_magno, z_parvo = W_p h_parvo
  • Loss: CE(z) + 0.3 · [CE(z_magno) + CE(z_parvo)]
  • AdamW 1e-3, warm-up + cosine, EMA, 50 epochs

+20 pp

optimiser, schedule, EMA + per-stream losses; BA averaging rule

+2 pp

own heads and w, at SB 0.75 and above (one seed)

+5–11 pp

luminance into parvo, only below SB 0.65

What stayed the same: both trunks are ResNet-18s, magno sees the dieted (blurred, centre-surround) luminance and parvo the L−M channel, and the streams meet only after layer 4. No earlier fusion: the BA's own Inter. variant, which mixed 3:1 after block 2, lost magno specialisation (probe bias 0.99 → 0.68). What changed is the ending. The BA averages the two layer-4 feature maps and feeds the mixture to one shared linear head with one loss. Because the head is linear, that equals averaging the two streams' logits through a tied head: neither stream is ever trained to classify on its own, and the tied head read magno alone at 0.19 top-1 in our frozen screen. Ours gives each stream its own linear head and its own cross-entropy (weight 0.3), so each logit scale is pinned by its own loss, and the decision is w times magno logits plus 1 − w times parvo logits. w is then a real dial: w = 0.5 / 0.7 / 0.85 give SB 0.69 / 0.77 / 0.81, and the magno head stays at SB 0.91 in every arm. Attribution, seed 0: the recipe (AdamW, cosine, EMA, auxiliary losses) is most of the gain, +20 pp with the BA's own averaging rule. Own heads plus w add about 2 pp at SB 0.75 and above. Giving parvo raw luminance adds 5 to 11 pp, but only at low shape bias; above SB 0.78 it falls below the averaging line. A hinge (magno vetoes parvo outside its top-3) added nothing over the weighted sum.
