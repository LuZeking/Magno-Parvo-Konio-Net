All new-recipe runs: one objective, average fusion w = 0.5, label smoothing 0.1, 50 epochs · cortex BA row = paper's dkl_bcs, 3 seeds

Five single-objective networks, all the BA's architecture and one cross-entropy on the output, against the BA TSN and the thesis's RGB ResNet-50. The new recipe with the BA's own fixed diet (three seeds) fixes most of the accuracy but loses about 0.08 of shape bias; eleven variants of that fixed-diet network, not shown, all sat between 0.64 and 0.67 at shape bias 0.59 to 0.65. Letting the diet grow from newborn to 48 months, the age of the BA's contrast filter, changes nothing. Letting it grow to the DVD adult endpoint, 300 months, is the first change that moves both axes: 0.683 at 0.687 over three seeds, with a steeper cortical gradient than the BA TSN. Holding the 300-month diet from the first step gives the most accurate network of the set, close to the RGB ResNet-50, and the least shape-biased one, with no stream split and no cortical gradient. The probe columns are the grouped readout probe with photos and textures held out (magno / parvo, shape minus texture accuracy). The cortex column is the paper's gradient, hV4 minus V1 stream dominance, mean over 8 NSD subjects; the recipe-only row is final once its third seed's encoding finishes.
