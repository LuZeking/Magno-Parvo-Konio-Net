This is the slide the story turns on. The control is identical to the new TSN in architecture, objective, recipe, seed and test input; the only difference is that its magno diet is held at 300 months from the first training step, so it never sees the blurred, low-contrast early input. It is the more accurate network (0.761, close to the RGB baseline's 0.757) and the least shape-biased one we have trained (0.371). Its magno stream reads texture on the held-out probe, and its joint features are the only ones of any run that are texture-dominant. In cortex its stream-dominance profile is flat and magno-dominated from V1 to hV4, and the readout weight that best predicts cortex rises from V1 to extrastriate, the pattern of the BA's no-diet network. So the thesis's claim that the diet gives the magno stream its shape bias survives, with a sharper mechanism: the shape bias comes from learning on coarse input first, which the network keeps after the input becomes adult. One seed so far; seeds 1 and 2 are the next GPU job.
