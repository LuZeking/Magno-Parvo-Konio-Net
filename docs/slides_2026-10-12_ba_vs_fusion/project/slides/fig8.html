The thesis's Fig. 8 measured each stream's representational shape bias with a cosine "triple correlation": the share of cue-conflict images whose last-layer activation is closer to the original shape image than to the original texture image (BA TSN: magno 0.986, parvo 0.113). That instrument needs the 160 original shape photos and 48 texture images, which live on the thesis author's old cluster (/share/klab/...) and are not here, so this slide uses the two stream-level measures we can run identically on both models. Left: behaviour of each stream read alone through the shared head, the BA's own readout; the magno stream is equally shape-biased in both models, the parvo stream stays below parity. Right: the paper's linear probe, shape minus texture decoding accuracy; the specialisation is the BA's in both models and survives holding out photos and textures, at about half the size.
