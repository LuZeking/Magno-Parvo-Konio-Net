The thesis's Fig. 8 measured each stream's representational shape bias with a cosine "triple correlation" that needs the 160 original shape photos and 48 texture images from the thesis author's old cluster, so this slide uses the two stream-level measures we can run identically on both models. Left: behaviour of each stream read alone through the shared head, the BA's own readout. The magno stream is still strongly shape-biased but less than in the BA TSN (0.85 against 0.91), and the parvo stream stays below parity (0.48). Right: the paper's linear probe, shape minus texture decoding accuracy. The magno-shape, parvo-texture split is there in all three seeds and survives holding out photos and textures; on this grouped readout probe the magno side is smaller than in the BA TSN (+0.33 against +0.49 held out) while the parvo side is unchanged (−0.24 against −0.23). On the paper's own Table 1 probe (exemplar pipeline, held out) the two networks are level: +0.316 against +0.337 for magno, −0.156 against −0.168 for parvo. An untrained network on the new TSN's test input sits at +0.087, the same as on undieted input, so the new TSN's magno lead is almost entirely learned (+0.23, against +0.13 for the BA). In the adult-diet control the magno stream reads texture (−0.19 held out), so the split itself depends on the developmental order.
