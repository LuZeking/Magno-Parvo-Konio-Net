Blue, dashed: training only. Each stream gets its own linear head and loss. Both are dropped at test time, so the network is the BA's and every BA analysis runs on it unchanged.

Grey, purple and gold boxes are the BA TSN exactly as the thesis describes it: the image converted to DKL, luminance through the developmental diet (Gaussian blur sigma 4 and the 48-month contrast-sensitivity filter) into the magno ResNet-18, the L−M channel undieted into the parvo ResNet-18, the two layer-4 feature maps averaged and read by one linear head over 112 MiniEcoset classes. The only architectural addition is the pair of blue heads: a linear classifier on each stream's pooled layer-4 features, trained with its own cross-entropy at weight 0.3 so that each stream gets direct supervision instead of only the gradient that passes through the averaged head. They never enter the decision and are not used at test time. Because the weights load into the BA's TwoStreamNetwork class unchanged, the thesis and paper tools evaluate the new TSN with no code change.
