Blue outline: the one change to the network's input. Blur and contrast filter follow the DVD curves at one age, rising linearly from newborn to adult; testing uses the adult end. No per-stream losses.

Grey, purple and gold boxes are the BA TSN as the thesis describes it: the image converted to DKL, luminance into the magno ResNet-18, the L−M channel undieted into the parvo ResNet-18, the two layer-4 feature maps averaged and read by one linear head over 112 MiniEcoset classes, trained with one cross-entropy on that head. The thesis diet mixes two ages: Gaussian blur sigma 4 is the DVD acuity of about a 3-month-old, its contrast-sensitivity filter is a 48-month-old's, and both are fixed for all of training. The new diet takes both stages from the DVD developmental curves at a single age (dkl_frontend.apply_dvd_diet), and that age grows linearly with the training step from 0 to 300 months over the 50 epochs, as DVD's own age curve does. Every evaluation uses 300 months, where the blur is inactive and the luminance is close to clean. The network may still carry a linear readout per stream for analysis, but those heads see detached features and send no gradient into the trunks, so the training signal is the single objective of the BA.
