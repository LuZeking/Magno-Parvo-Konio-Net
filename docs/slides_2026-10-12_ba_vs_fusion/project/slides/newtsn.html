Both models on one instrument: our 1,200-image cue-conflict set and the paper's linear probe · thesis checkpoint dkl_blur_cs_dvd_trans

Every behavioural and representational number is measured the same way for both models: the BA thesis checkpoint scored by our cue-conflict pipeline (results/cue_conflict/thesis_dvd_trans.json), the new TSN by the Stage-1 evaluation, whose front end matches that pipeline exactly. On that common footing the thesis TSN's shape bias is 0.697, slightly above the 0.678 the thesis reported on its 1,280-image set. "Magno alone" and "parvo alone" read each stream through the shared head, the BA's own readout. The probe rows are shape minus texture decoding accuracy; "as in the BA" shares exemplars between training and test folds like the thesis's Table 1, "held-out exemplars" keeps test photos and textures unseen. The new TSN is seed 0 of the Stage-1 averaging arm; seeds 1 and 2 are queued on the agcichy A5000s (array 26627723, about 10 hours each once they start).
