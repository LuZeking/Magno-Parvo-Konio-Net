Behaviour: the BA thesis checkpoint scored by our cue-conflict pipeline (results/cue_conflict/thesis_dvd_trans.json), the new TSN by the Stage-2 evaluation with the identical front end; ± is the SD over three seeds. Magno alone and parvo alone read each stream through the shared head, the BA's own readout. The probe rows are shape minus texture decoding accuracy with test photos and textures held out, on two instruments: the paper's Table 1 probe (exemplar pipeline, means of three seeds; the BA column is the paper's dkl_bcs) and the grouped readout probe of the deck's figures. On the paper's probe the new TSN's split is the BA's, +0.316 against +0.337 for magno and −0.156 against −0.168 for parvo; on the grouped readout the magno side is smaller. The cortex rows are the paper's Fig. 3 measures from the banded-ridge NSD encoding: the gradient is the change in which stream adds more unique variance from V1 to hV4 (negative = towards parvo), and r²/NC is the median prediction accuracy relative to the noise ceiling. The BA column there is the paper's dkl_bcs (3 seeds), the same thesis diet and network. The magno stream is the one that lost something: its standalone shape bias drops from 0.91 to 0.85 and its probe lead from +0.49 to +0.33, while the parvo stream is as texture-leaning as before.
