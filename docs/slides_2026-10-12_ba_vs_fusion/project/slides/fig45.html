Our re-runs of the BA recipe show why the reported 0.469 is low: accuracy swings between roughly 0.35 and 0.60 from epoch to epoch, and the thesis reports whichever value the last epoch happens to hit. The new recipe rises smoothly, peaks at 0.681 at epoch 26 and ends at 0.666; the late drift of 1.5 points is mild overfitting, not instability.
