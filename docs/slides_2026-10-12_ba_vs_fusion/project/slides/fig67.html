Right, the thesis's Fig. 7: each stream read out by a linear classifier on its last convolutional layer. The new TSN's readouts are linear heads trained on detached features, so they never shape the trunks. Both streams carry more category information than in the BA TSN, the magno stream most (0.24 to 0.39), and the parvo stream remains the more predictive one, as the thesis described. A frozen 1-epoch readout on the new TSN would make the comparison with the thesis exact.
