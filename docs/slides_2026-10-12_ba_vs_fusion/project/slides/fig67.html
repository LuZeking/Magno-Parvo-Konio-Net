Left, the thesis's Fig. 6: on the same 1,200 cue-conflict images the BA TSN and the new TSN are indistinguishable, shape bias 0.697 against 0.700 and shape accuracy 0.360 against 0.355. The 20-point gain is on natural images; cue-conflict decisions did not change. That is what backs the BA story: the behaviour the thesis reported was not an artefact of an undertrained model. Right, the thesis's Fig. 7: each stream read out by a linear classifier on its last convolutional layer. Both streams of the new TSN carry more category information, and the pattern the thesis described survives: the parvo stream is the more predictive one, the magno stream the shape-biased one. The readouts differ in one respect: the thesis trained a fresh linear classifier for one epoch on frozen features, while ours is the head each stream was trained with; a frozen 1-epoch readout on the new TSN would make the comparison exact.
