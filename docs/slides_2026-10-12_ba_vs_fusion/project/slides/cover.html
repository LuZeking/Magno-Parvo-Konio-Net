A new main TSN: the BA's model, retrained

Same network at test time. Top-1 rises from 0.469 to 0.666; shape bias (0.70) and the magno/parvo split are unchanged.

New TSN = Stage-1 arm A0, seed 0 · seeds 1–2 training · thesis = BA_Manocchio_Benito.pdf

The BA's weakness was accuracy: its TSN (DKL B&CS) reached 0.469 top-1, 20 points below the DVD models. The new main TSN is the same network at test time, two ResNet-18 streams averaged into one linear head, trained with a modern recipe plus a training-only loss per stream. It reaches 0.666, level with the DVD baseline (0.672) and DKL Base (0.673), while its shape bias and stream specialisation match the BA TSN on the same instruments. So the BA's story stands on a model that now performs at the level of its comparison networks.
