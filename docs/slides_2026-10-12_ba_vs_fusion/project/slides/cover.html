A new main TSN: the BA's network, raised on a developmental diet

Same network at test time, one objective. Top-1 rises from 0.469 to 0.683 at the BA's shape bias (0.69 vs 0.70); the magno/parvo split holds and the cortical gradient is steeper.

New TSN = fs2_dvd300_avg_w050_ls, seeds 0–2 · control = fs2_dvdfix300_avg_w050_ls · thesis = BA_Manocchio_Benito.pdf

The BA's weakness was accuracy: its TSN (DKL B&CS) reached 0.469 top-1, 20 points below the DVD models. The new main TSN is the same network at test time, two ResNet-18 streams averaged into one linear head and trained with one cross-entropy on that head, no per-stream losses. Two things changed: a modern training recipe, and the magno diet, which now follows the DVD developmental curves from birth to adulthood over the course of training instead of holding the thesis's fixed mix of a 3-month blur and a 48-month contrast filter. Over three seeds it reaches 0.683 top-1 at shape bias 0.687, the BA TSN's shape bias at 21 points more accuracy. A control that sees the adult diet from the first step, the same test input, loses the shape bias, the stream split and the cortical gradient, so the developmental order does the work, not the input the network ends on.
