BA recipe: thesis §3.3.5 · new recipe: _stage2 in src/training/standalone_trainer.py · recipe-only arm = fs2_bcs_avg_w050_ls, 3 seeds

The thesis trained every model for 150 epochs with Adam at a constant learning rate of 1e-4. Under that recipe the dieted two-stream models never settle: validation accuracy swings by about 0.1 between epochs, and the last epoch, which the thesis reports, lands anywhere in that band. The new recipe is a standard modern one: AdamW at a higher peak learning rate with weight decay, a short warm-up, cosine decay to zero, evaluation on an exponential moving average of the weights, and label smoothing 0.1, all in 50 epochs. The two changes are separated by a recipe-only arm: the same recipe with the BA's fixed diet (fs2_bcs_avg_w050_ls, three seeds) reaches 0.665 at shape bias 0.620, so the recipe gives about 20 of the 21 points of accuracy and the developmental diet gives the rest plus 0.07 of shape bias. Eleven single-objective variants of the fixed diet (readout weights, stream balancing, shorter schedules) all stayed between 0.64 and 0.67 at shape bias 0.59 to 0.65; the developmental diet is the only change that moved both axes.
