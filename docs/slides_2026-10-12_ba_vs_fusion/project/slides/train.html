BA recipe: thesis §3.3.5 · new recipe: STAGE1_RECIPE in src/training/standalone_trainer.py

The thesis trained every model for 150 epochs with Adam at a constant learning rate of 1e-4. Under that recipe the dieted two-stream models never settle: validation accuracy swings by about 0.1 from one epoch to the next, and the last epoch, which is what the thesis reports, lands anywhere in that band. The new recipe is a standard modern one: AdamW at a higher peak learning rate with weight decay, a short warm-up, cosine decay to zero, and evaluation on an exponential moving average of the weights, all in 50 epochs. The one addition specific to the two-stream design is the per-stream loss: each stream's own linear head is trained with cross-entropy at weight 0.3, so the magno stream receives direct supervision rather than only the gradient that survives the averaging. We have not run the ablation that separates the loss from the optimiser changes; the earlier finding that the end-of-training instability hit only the dieted two-stream models suggests the per-stream loss matters, but that is untested.
