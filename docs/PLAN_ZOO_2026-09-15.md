# Pre-registration: the readout account across the field's model zoo (W3)

Written 2026-09-15, 22:15, before any zoo network was extracted (W3 of
`docs/PLAN_NMI9_2026-09-15.md`). Extends `docs/PLAN_SUBSPACE_2026-09-15.md` (W1), whose
rule, estimators and gate are used unchanged. Committed before `slurm/zoo_cc.sh` runs.

## Question

Fig. 6b shows, in six ImageNet networks, that one gain on a network's own shape-predictive
subspace moves its cue-conflict shape-bias rate by 0.25–0.33 within 1.5 points of top-1, and
that the six can be re-ordered. The ML reader's question is what this does to the ranking the
field actually publishes, and whether a network *trained* to be shape-biased (Stylized-ImageNet)
carries its bias in the representation or in the readout's operating point. W3 measures three
numbers per network — the trained rate, the reachable span within an accuracy budget, and a
probe-based representational bias — across the torchvision zoo, and asks how the ranking by
the trained rate survives the readout.

## Design

**Networks (42).** torchvision `IMAGENET1K_V1` weights unless stated:
- the six of Fig. 6, reused from the stored `2026-09-14_gen` run: ResNet-50, ResNet-152, VGG-16,
  ViT-B/16, ConvNeXt-S, ConvNeXt-B;
- 32 new: AlexNet, VGG-11-BN, VGG-16-BN, VGG-19-BN, GoogLeNet, Inception-v3, ResNet-18/34/101,
  ResNeXt-50 32×4d, ResNeXt-101 32×8d, Wide-ResNet-50-2, Wide-ResNet-101-2, DenseNet-121/201,
  MobileNet-V2, MobileNet-V3-L, ShuffleNet-V2 ×1.0, MNASNet-1.0, EfficientNet-B0/B3,
  EfficientNet-V2-S, RegNet-Y-8GF/16GF, ConvNeXt-T/L, ViT-B/32, ViT-L/16, Swin-T/S/B, MaxViT-T;
- one training-recipe variant of a fixed architecture: ResNet-50 `IMAGENET1K_V2`;
- three shape-biased controls from Geirhos et al. (2019), all ResNet-50: trained on
  Stylized-ImageNet (SIN), on SIN + IN, and on SIN + IN then fine-tuned on IN
  (`hub:resnet50_sin`, `hub:resnet50_sin_in`, `hub:resnet50_sin_in_ft`; checkpoints fetched
  2026-09-15 from the authors' repository).

**Per network, exactly the W1 pipeline** (`src/analysis/generality`): penultimate features y of
the 1,200 cue-conflict images (y = the input of the network's last linear layer, captured on the
network's own forward pass and checked against its logits); the 16-class Geirhos decision rule;
shape- and texture-predictive subspaces (PCA-256 → 16-class LDA → 15 directions each → Löwdin
orthogonalisation) on one stratified half, fixed-head gain sweep y(w) = P_R y + 2w P_S y +
2(1 − w) P_T y scored on the other half, 10 splits both directions; the pre-specified unit rule
(median split by η²_shape − η²_texture) for comparison; the accuracy gate on the same stratified
10-per-class ILSVRC-2012 validation subset (10,000 images), all-image bases, w kept if top-1 is
within 5 points of the trained top-1; gated span = max − min of the seed-mean curve within the
gate. Preprocessing is Resize 256 / CenterCrop 224 as in Fig. 6 for every network trained at
224; the weights' own sizes for the three that were not (Inception-v3 342/299, EfficientNet-B3
320/300, EfficientNet-V2-S 384/384).

**New measure: the representational bias.** The paper's linear probe (Methods 4.3): PCA to 256
components of y over the 1,200 images, multinomial logistic regression (C = 1.0) under 5-fold
stratified cross-validation predicting the shape class and, separately, the texture class;
probe bias = shape accuracy − texture accuracy on the same folds (`probe_bias.py`, the estimator
of `src/analysis/behavior/stream_shape_texture.py` applied to a single feature vector).

**Ranking statistics**, over the 39 ImageNet-trained networks (the three SIN controls and the
recipe variant are analysed separately; the V1 ResNet-50 represents its architecture):
- the rate at the *shape end* of each network's gate (its largest gated w) and at the *texture
  end* (its smallest gated w);
- Kendall τ between the ranking by trained rate and the ranking by shape-end rate, and by
  texture-end rate; the number of pairwise order reversals among the 741 pairs between the
  trained and the shape-end ranking;
- Kendall τ between the ranking by trained rate and the ranking by probe bias;
- Spearman ρ between gated span and trained rate, and between gated span and trained top-1
  (descriptive).

## Predictions, written before running

- **P-Z1 (the published ranking is not invariant to the readout).** Kendall τ between the
  trained ranking and the shape-end ranking is < 0.7, and at least 25% of the 741 pairs reverse
  order. Failure: τ ≥ 0.85 — the claim narrows to magnitude and the re-ordering is dropped from
  the abstract.
- **P-Z2 (magnitude generalises across the zoo).** The gated span is ≥ 0.20 in at least 75% of
  the 39 networks, and the whole sweep lies inside the gate in at least half of them. Failure:
  fewer than 50% reach 0.20 — the abstract's "six frozen ImageNet networks" stays as it is and
  the zoo is reported as the range.
- **P-Z3 (Stylized-ImageNet control).** (a) The SIN-trained ResNet-50 has the highest trained
  rate of all 42; (b) its gated span is ≥ 0.20 in the same direction (w → 1 raises the rate), so
  that training on SIN moves the operating point but does not remove the readout-dependence;
  (c) its probe bias exceeds the probe bias of every ImageNet-trained network — the
  representation is also more shape-informative, and the rate alone cannot tell the two apart.
  Each part is reported on its own; (b) failing with a span < 0.10 would mean SIN's bias is
  representational rather than a readout property, and would be stated as such.
- **P-Z4 (the three numbers dissociate).** Kendall τ between the ranking by trained rate and the
  ranking by probe bias is < 0.7 across the 39 networks. Failure: τ ≥ 0.85 — then the
  "how to report shape bias" box loses its third line.

Descriptives without thresholds: span against architecture family (convolutional against
attention-based), against top-1 and against feature dimensionality; the ResNet-50 V1 → V2
recipe difference in trained rate against the readout span of either; the unit-rule gated span
beside the subspace span for every network (P-D of the subspace plan, extended without a
threshold).

Statistics as Methods 4.7: no p-values are attached to τ or ρ over networks (networks are not a
random sample of anything; the numbers are descriptive of this zoo); every prediction is
reported whether met or not, and no network is dropped after the fact.

## Compute (CPU, `main`/`standard`, ≤ 32G, `%A_%a` logs)

`slurm/zoo_cc.sh`: 42 elements (`--array=0-41%6`), one network each: extraction of 1,200 +
10,000 images (≈ 20 min for ResNet-50-sized networks, ≈ 1 h for ViT-L/16), then
`subspace_factor`, `unit_factor`, `gain_accuracy` (both rules) and `probe_bias`, minutes. Outputs
under `/scratch/byrong/mpk/generality/{features,behaviour}/2026-09-15_zoo/`; the six Fig. 6
networks are symlinked from `2026-09-14_gen`. Aggregation:
`tmp_analysis/figs_v2/zoo_behaviour.py` → `results/figures_v2/zoo_behaviour.json`, which
evaluates P-Z1–P-Z4 and prints one row per network.

## Deliverables

Extended Data Table 16 (one row per network: D, top-1, trained rate, gate w-range, texture-end
and shape-end rates, gated span under both rules, probe bias); a zoo panel for Fig. 6b or a
new Extended Data figure (trained rank against shape-end rank; span against trained rate with

the three numbers and the gated span for any torchvision network; one Discussion paragraph

## Outcomes

Appended 2026-09-15, 23:35, after arrays 26492416 (elements 11–41, prio) and 26492422 (0–10,
standard; the prio queue's per-user submit cap forced the split); all 42 elements completed
(≤ 41 min, ≤ 4.9 GB each). Source `results/figures_v2/zoo_behaviour.json`
(`tmp_analysis/figs_v2/zoo_behaviour.py`); Extended Data Table 16, Extended Data Fig. 6.

*Correction to the Design section:* the list holds **38** ImageNet-trained networks (six of
Fig. 6 plus 32 new), not 39, so the pairwise statistics run over 703 pairs, not 741. Nothing was
dropped or added; the count above was a slip when the plan was written.

- **P-Z1 met.** Kendall τ between the ranking by trained rate and by the shape-end rate is 0.47
  (by the texture-end rate 0.78); 188 of 703 pairs (27%) reverse order.
- **P-Z2 met.** Gated span 0.20–0.50 (median 0.30); ≥ 0.20 in 37 of 38 (EfficientNet-V2-S
  0.198); the whole sweep lies inside the 5-point gate in 38 of 38 (largest change of top-1
  inside the gate 4.4 points, MobileNet-V2; median 0.7). ρ(w, rate) ≥ 0.99 in every network.
  Descriptives: Spearman ρ between span and trained rate +0.04, between span and top-1 −0.22;
  mean span 0.31 in convolutional and 0.27 in attention-based networks; largest spans AlexNet
  0.50, MobileNet-V3-L 0.44, MNASNet 0.40; smallest EfficientNet-V2-S 0.20, Swin-B 0.23.
  Trained rates run from 0.10 (VGG-11-BN) to 0.62 (ViT-B/32, a 7 × 7 token grid at 224 px).
  The Fig. 6 statement that every network can be moved past every other's trained rate does
  not extend to the whole zoo: only 2 of 38 reach ViT-B/32's 0.62 at the shape end.
- **P-Z3: (a) met, (b) failed, (c) met.** (a) The SIN-trained ResNet-50 has the highest trained
  rate of all 42 (0.81; next ViT-B/32, 0.62). (b) Its gated span is 0.17 (0.70 → 0.87, ρ 0.99,
  top-1 58.2% with a 3.9-point change inside the gate) — below the pre-registered 0.20 and below
  every ImageNet-trained network. (c) Its probe bias is −0.11 against −0.30 to −0.66 for the
  38. Reading, as pre-stated for a failure of (b): Stylized-ImageNet training puts most of the
  bias into the representation rather than into the readout's operating point — the one
  network in the zoo whose rate is closer to a representational property, and exactly the case
  the rate alone cannot distinguish from the others while the probe does. The SIN + IN network
  (trained 0.36, span 0.35, probe −0.40) and the SIN + IN network fine-tuned on IN (0.23, 0.28,
  −0.58) return stepwise to the zoo.
- **P-Z4 met.** τ between the ranking by trained rate and by probe bias is 0.44 (Spearman ρ
  0.59) over the 38.
- **Recipe variant.** ResNet-50 V1 → V2: trained rate 0.22 → 0.19, gated span 0.29 → 0.33,
  probe bias −0.57 → −0.62, top-1 76.0 → 81.4%: the recipe moves the rate by 0.04 where the
  readout moves it by 0.3.
- **Unit rule.** The pre-specified median split reaches 0.03–0.22 within the gate; the subspace
  rule exceeds it in 38 of 38 networks (P-D of the subspace plan, extended without threshold).
- **Post hoc addition (not pre-registered), 2026-09-16: error consistency.** Cohen's κ with each
  of the ten observers (Results 2.5's estimator, `src/analysis/generality/error_consistency.py`,
  ceiling recomputed at 0.234) at the trained readout and at the two ends of each network's gate.
  Over the 38 ImageNet-trained networks: 2.4–23.0% of the ceiling at the trained readout (median
  7.9%; ViT-B/32 highest, ResNet-50 V2 recipe lowest at 0.5%); ρ(κ, trained rate) = +0.80; κ is
  higher at the shape end than at the texture end of the gate in 38 of 38 (median +13.5 points of
  the ceiling, max +25), and the best gated readout reaches 36.7% (ViT-B/32 at w = 0.95). The SIN
  ResNet-50 reaches 39.5% trained and 53.3% at its shape end; SIN + IN 11.7% → 18.5%; fine-tuned
  5.9% → 14.7%. Under this pipeline's preprocessing VGG-16 gives 3.4%, against 7.3% from the
  published response file (Extended Data Table 7), as its trained rate differs (0.110 v 0.172).
  Reading: agreement does track the rate across networks and along a gate — the Discussion's
  "the rate does not identify a decision process" is softened to "a weak proxy" — but no
  ImageNet-trained readout exceeds a third of the ceiling.
