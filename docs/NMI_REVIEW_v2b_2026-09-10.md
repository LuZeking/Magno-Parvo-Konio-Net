### Summary of key messages
Unchanged from the prior report: a two-stream network fed luminance and L−M channels under a fixed developmental blur develops oppositely biased streams by different routes; fitted to eight NSD subjects, the texture-biased stream's unique variance rises toward hV4 along a gradient that is the most extreme of all 630 ROI labellings and that training without the diet erodes; behaviour reads out from both streams and its rate is moved across 0.40–0.90 by the readout weight over frozen features, surviving a refitted nonlinear head; robustness, human trial-level agreement and dimensionality do not follow from the rate.

### Major comments
1. **Decided-image denominator (§2.2, Fig. 2d; Methods 4.3).** Unchanged from the prior report: please add a forced shape-vs-texture estimator and per-readout shape and texture accuracies.
2. **Scope of the readout claim (§2.4).** Unchanged: please test re-weighting on a frozen single-trunk network.
3. **Single-seed arms.** Unchanged; two further seeds for the two cortical controls would settle the "degenerate decomposition" statement.
4. **Title.** Unchanged.
5. **Length and placement (§2.3, Abstract).** The restored nuisance-axis and estimator paragraphs are exactly the material a referee needs, and they are now in the right paper; they are in the wrong place. Two sentences in §2.3 with the numbers, and the full account in Extended Data beside ED Fig. 3 and ED Tables 8–9, would serve the reader better and bring the main text back toward the format. The abstract should be cut to ≈ 200 words.
6. **Post hoc status of the gradient (§2.3).** Please state, where the exact null is introduced, that it calibrates the extremity of a survivor from seven tested hypotheses and does not test a prediction.
